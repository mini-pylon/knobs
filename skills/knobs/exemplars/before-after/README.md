# before-after

## When

A group that tunes how an element responds to hover or focus.

## What makes it good

- **Look:** both sides are labelled.
- **Look:** both are drawn at one scale over one baseline.
- **Look:** the difference reads at a glance.
- **Behaviour:** change `scale` and only the hover side changes.

## Adapting

Rename the group and its keys to the project's own. Draw the project's card shape in place of the stand-in box. Keep the shared scale, the baseline and the labels.
