# timeline-zones

## When

A group that shapes scrolling or paging through sections.

## What makes it good

- **Look:** drawn to scale, with blocks of equal width and zones exactly `snapWidth` of a section.
- **Look:** a tick marks each centre.
- **Behaviour:** the marker moves with the position. Two screenshots a second apart differ only in the marker's place.
- **Behaviour:** turn `snap` off and the zones disappear while the ticks stay.

## Adapting

Rename the group and its keys to the project's own. Replace the stand-in position with the page's scroll progress. Keep the blocks, the zones, the ticks and the end labels.
